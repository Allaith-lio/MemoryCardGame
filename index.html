Card-Game

Rule 1 : Flip two cards. On your turn, flip over any two cards so everyone can see them
Rule 2 : Keep matching pairs. If the cards match, you keep the pair and get another turn
Rule 3 : Flip back if they don't match. If they do not match, turn them face down again, and it becomes the next player's turn.
Important note⚡: Points are calculated based on the number of clicks , the fewer the clicks , the greater the winnings.


As the difficulty level increases, the time decreases; therefore, choose the difficulty level carefully
